# Marketplace Fees in Australia Compared: Amazon, eBay, Etsy & More

URL: https://ecomchannels.com/insights/australian-marketplace-fees-compared/
Category: Marketplaces & multichannel
Updated: 2026-10-30

> Published seller fees for Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Kogan, Temu and retailer marketplaces in Australia, plus the hidden costs that decide if a channel pays.

Marketplace fees in Australia range from almost nothing upfront, as with Temu's advertised 0% commission and eBay's free selling for smaller sellers, to category commissions of around 6% to 15% on Amazon and a monthly store fee plus commission on retailer marketplaces. The headline rate is only the start. Payment fees, advertising, fulfilment, returns and performance penalties often decide whether a channel makes money.

## Amazon Australia

Amazon charges a selling plan fee plus a referral fee on every sale:

- **Plan:** [Individual at $0.99 (excl. GST) per unit sold, or Professional at $49.95 (excl. GST) per month](https://sell.amazon.com.au/pricing) (Amazon, 2026).
- **Referral fee:** [most categories fall between 6% and 15%](https://sell.amazon.com.au/pricing), calculated on the total sale price including delivery, with no minimum or maximum.
- **Examples after the August 2025 restructure:** [Home & Kitchen 13%, Electronic Accessories 13.5%, Consumer Electronics 8%, and Media 15%](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/help/hub/reference/external/GNB3EQ3QBS4ZVGF2) (Amazon, 2025). Some categories use price tiers. Beauty, Health & Personal Care, for example, is 8% at $20 or less and 13% above $20.
- **Extras:** Fulfilment by Amazon, storage, advertising and optional programs are charged separately.

Because the referral fee applies to delivery charges too, how you price shipping affects what you pay. More detail is in our [Amazon Australia guide](https://ecomchannels.com/insights/selling-on-amazon-australia-retailer-guide/).

## eBay Australia

eBay's structure depends on your sales volume:

- **Small sellers:** Australian sellers with [up to $25,000 in sales over the past 12 months can sell without final value fees](https://www.ebay.com.au/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822) under eBay's free selling experience. Fees still apply to optional listing upgrades and items delivered overseas, and these sellers generally need to use eBay's shipping labels (eBay, 2026).
- **Above $25,000:** sellers move automatically to the **Pro Starter** plan, which has no monthly fee but charges a percentage final value fee that varies by category tier, plus a fixed order fee of $0.30 (incl. GST).
- **Pro Basic, Featured and Anchor** plans cost [$27.45, $82.45 and $604.95 a month (incl. GST)](https://www.ebay.com.au/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/ebay-stores-selling-fees-managed-payments-sellers?id=4809) and offer lower final value fees in most categories.
- **Penalty fees:** sellers rated Below Standard pay an [extra 5.5% (incl. GST) on sales, rising to 6.6% after four consecutive months](https://www.ebay.com.au/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/ebay-stores-selling-fees-managed-payments-sellers?id=4809) (eBay, 2026).

eBay's fees are quoted including GST, while Amazon's are quoted excluding GST. Keep that in mind when comparing.

## Etsy

Etsy suits handmade, vintage and craft supplies. Its fees are simple: [a $0.20 USD listing fee per listing and a 6.5% transaction fee on the total order including shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics), plus payment processing fees that vary by country (Etsy, 2026). Listing fees are charged whether or not the item sells, and again when a sold listing auto-renews. Etsy Ads and Offsite Ads are extra.

## Kogan and Temu

**Kogan Marketplace** does not publish its commission rates publicly; they are set in the seller agreement. It does publish a [Merchant Payment Processing Fee of 20c plus 1.39% per order](https://help.kogansellers.com/s/article/Merchant-Payment-Processing-Fee) (Kogan, 2026).

**Temu's** Australian seller centre advertises [0% commission fees and free registration](https://au.seller.temu.com/login.html?login_scene=302) for local sellers (Temu, 2026). The trade-off is pricing pressure. Temu's shoppers expect very low prices, so your margin is set by what you can charge, not by fees.

## Retailer marketplaces

Woolworths MarketPlus (BIG W Market and Everyday Market), Bunnings, Kmart, Myer, THE ICONIC, Temple & Webster and Baby Bunting generally negotiate terms with each seller rather than publishing a rate card. The Woolworths MarketPlus promotional terms show the typical structure: [category sales commissions, payment processing charges, monthly store fees, returns and claims costs, fulfilment and freight, and optional paid media](https://portal.woolworthsmarketplus.com.au/auspost-campaign-terms-and-conditions) (Woolworths Group, 2026). Ask each marketplace for its commission by category, store fee, payment terms and chargeback rules before you commit.

## The costs that don't appear on the rate card

To compare channels properly, calculate contribution margin per order:

**Sale price − commission − payment/order fees − advertising − fulfilment and freight − packaging − expected returns and refunds − penalties − product cost**

The items most often missed:

- **Advertising.** Sponsored placements are close to essential on large marketplaces and can match or exceed the commission.
- **Returns.** Free-return expectations differ by marketplace and category.
- **Performance penalties.** eBay's 5.5% Below Standard fee is a published example. On other marketplaces, poor metrics cost you visibility instead.
- **Settlement gaps.** Fees taken at settlement can differ from what you expected because of category reassignment, refunds or adjustments. Without reconciliation, these errors go unnoticed.

## FAQ

### Which Australian marketplace has the lowest fees?

On published rates, eBay is free for sellers under $25,000 a year, and Temu advertises 0% commission. Low fees don't guarantee profit. Compare contribution margin per order after advertising, freight, returns and the prices you can achieve on each channel.

### Are marketplace fees charged on shipping?

Often, yes. Amazon's referral fee, eBay's final value fee and Etsy's transaction fee all include delivery charges in the amount the fee is calculated on.

### Do marketplace fees include GST?

It varies. Amazon quotes fees excluding GST, while eBay quotes them including GST. Check each marketplace's tax invoice and get advice on claiming GST credits for your business.
