TikTok Shop and Live Shopping: A Guide for Australian Sellers

TikTok Shop is still not open to Australian buyers. What that means for local sellers, how live shopping performs overseas and how to prepare now.5 min read7 cited sourcesUpdated 30 October 2026

Key figureTikTok Shop is live for buyers in 24 markets, not including AustraliaSource: synlighetsverket.se

TikTok Shop is not yet available to Australian shoppers or local sellers, but Australian brands can already sell through it into overseas markets such as the US and UK. As of 14 October 2026, TikTok Shop was open to buyers in 24 markets, and Australia was not one of them. The sensible plan is to build a TikTok content habit and clean product data now, use the platform to drive traffic to your store and marketplaces, and be ready if a local launch is confirmed.

Where TikTok Shop stands in Australia

TikTok's local leadership has been clear that a launch is not imminent. At TikTok Spotlight in Sydney in March 2026, Amy Bradshaw, GM Global Business Solutions ANZ, said she was "not aware of any plans to launch TikTok Shop in Australia". Marketing-Interactive noted that a launch needs local logistics, fulfilment partners, payment integrations and a seller network.

There has been speculation since. A Chinese trade report in August 2026 suggested Australia could follow in the first quarter of 2027, but it relies on anonymous sources and has not been confirmed by TikTok. Treat any date as unconfirmed until TikTok opens seller registration for Australia.

Meanwhile the audience is already here. TikTok's advertising tools showed 10.9 million users aged 18 and over in Australia in late 2025, reaching 51.2% of adults, and TikTok says more than 350,000 Australian businesses use the platform.

Australian brands are selling on TikTok Shop overseas

The cross-border route is real and proven for some categories. TikTok reports that Hismile, which first launched on TikTok Shop in 2023, now operates in 16 markets and is expected to generate $50 million in GMV on TikTok Shop in 2026. The same TikTok post says more than one in three major AUNZ advertisers on TikTok now invest overseas.

These are platform-reported results for brands built around short video, so they are not typical outcomes. They do show what cross-border selling requires: a registered presence in the target market, local shipping and returns, creator partnerships and a high volume of content.

For most Australian SMB retailers, the more practical step is using TikTok as a demand engine that sends shoppers to an Australian checkout: your own store or a marketplace listing.

What live shopping is and how it performs

Live shopping combines a livestream with instant purchase and real-time chat. McKinsey's analysis of early adopters found companies reporting conversion rates approaching 30%, up to ten times higher than conventional e-commerce. Those figures came largely from China and from company-reported results in 2020 and 2021, so treat them as an upper bound rather than a forecast.

McKinsey also cautions that live events work well for awareness and trial but less well for loyalty, and that many impulse buyers are one-off customers. Apparel and fashion dominated livestream categories, followed by beauty and food.

Australian interest is moderate. DHL found 46% of Australian shoppers are interested or somewhat interested in live-streamed shopping events, below the global average. Without native TikTok Shop checkout, Australian live sessions usually rely on links to a store or marketplace, which adds a step.

How to prepare now

  1. Build the content habit first. TikTok rewards frequent, native-feeling video. Work out a sustainable cadence and test which product angles get saves and shares.
  2. Clean up product data. TikTok Shop, like marketplaces, needs accurate titles, attributes, images and variants. Fixing this once in a master record helps every channel.
  3. Send traffic somewhere that converts. Link videos and live sessions to fast product pages or marketplace listings with clear delivery and returns information.
  4. Test a live format small. A short weekly product demo with a discount code shows whether your audience responds before you invest in production.
  5. Consider cross-border only with a plan for fulfilment. Selling into the US or UK through TikTok Shop means meeting local delivery, returns and compliance rules.

FAQ

Is TikTok Shop available in Australia?

Not as of October 2026. Australian shoppers cannot buy through TikTok Shop and Australian businesses cannot register as local sellers. TikTok has not confirmed a launch date; reports of an early-2027 launch rely on anonymous sources.

Can an Australian business sell on TikTok Shop?

Yes, into markets where TikTok Shop operates, such as the US and UK, if the business meets that market's seller requirements, including local shipping and returns. TikTok reports that some Australian brands already do this.

Does live shopping work in Australia without TikTok Shop?

It can, but with more friction. Live sessions on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube can link to your store or marketplace listings. Start with short, regular demos and a tracked discount code so you can measure sales from each session.
