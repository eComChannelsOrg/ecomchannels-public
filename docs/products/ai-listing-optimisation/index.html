Features

Already trained on every marketplace’s rules

Title lengths, bullet counts, banned words, required attributes and category rules are already trained into the AI for each marketplace, so there is no set-up before your first listing.

AI drafts, people approve

AI produces the first draft from your product data. Nothing goes live until a reviewer approves it, and every approved version is kept.

One product record

Bulk updates

Questions about AI listing optimisation

Which marketplaces is the listing AI trained for?

It is already trained on the listing rules of Amazon, TikTok Shop, Temu, Woolworths Everyday Market, BIG W Market, Bunnings Marketplace, Kmart Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, Myer Marketplace, THE ICONIC, Lasoo, Temple & Webster, Baby Bunting and Kogan, plus Shopify, WooCommerce and eComChannels storefronts.

Does AI publish listings automatically?

No. The AI drafts to each marketplace’s trained rules, and a person on your team approves them before they are published.
