## Features

### Already trained on every marketplace’s rules
Title lengths, bullet counts, banned words, required attributes and category rules are already trained into the AI for each marketplace, so there is no set-up before your first listing.

### AI drafts, people approve
AI produces the first draft from your product data. Nothing goes live until a reviewer approves it, and every approved version is kept.

### One product record
A single master product feeds every channel, so a correction is made once and flows to every listing.

### Keyword and content research
AI-assisted product research suggests the terms shoppers use, to help listings be found in marketplace search.

### Bulk updates
Optimise a whole range at once instead of editing listings one marketplace at a time.

### Consistent brand voice
Copy follows your own style guide across Amazon, eBay, Kogan, Bunnings and your web store.
