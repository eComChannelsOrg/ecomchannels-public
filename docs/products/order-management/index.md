## Features

### One order queue
Orders from every marketplace and store arrive in one queue to be allocated, picked, packed and shipped.

### Inventory and warehouses
One stock position per SKU across warehouses, pushed to every channel to prevent overselling.

### Pricing
Price books and pricing rules per channel and country, including tax handling.

### Purchasing
Purchase orders, goods receiving and supplier information next to the products they belong to.

### B2B and wholesale
Serve trade customers from the same catalogue and stock as your retail channels.

### Customer service
A shared team inbox with the order in view, so support can answer without switching systems.

## Questions about Order management (OMS)

### What does an order management system (OMS) do?
An OMS collects orders from every place you sell, keeps one accurate stock figure per product and runs fulfilment, with tracking sent back to each sales channel.

### Does eComChannels prevent overselling?
Stock is held centrally per SKU and the available quantity is pushed to each connected channel, so a sale on one marketplace reduces what is offered on the others.
