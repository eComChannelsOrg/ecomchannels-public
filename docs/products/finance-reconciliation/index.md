# Finance auto reconciliation for marketplaces and online retailers

URL: https://ecomchannels.com/products/finance-reconciliation/
Updated: 2026-10-30

> Reconcile marketplace remittances and settlements against orders automatically, with fee rules and reports. Amazon, eBay, Kogan, Bunnings and more.

Marketplaces pay in batches, net of commissions and fees. eComChannels imports each remittance, matches every line to its order, applies your fee and settlement rules and shows what is paid, short-paid or missing.

matched · short-paid · unmatched

## Features

### Remittance import
Upload or import remittance and settlement files from each marketplace, with a saved import layout per channel.

### Automatic order matching
Each payout line is matched to its sales order and invoice, so only the exceptions need a person.

### Fee and settlement rules
Commission, fee and settlement rules per channel explain the difference between the sale and the payout.

### Exceptions first
Short payments, unmatched lines and unpaid orders are flagged for follow-up.

### Finance dashboard and reports
See receipts, fees and outstanding amounts by channel and period.

### Invoices from orders
Invoices are generated from sales orders, so finance works from the same data as operations.

## Questions about Finance auto reconciliation

### What does marketplace reconciliation mean?
Checking that every order sold on a marketplace was paid for, at the right amount after commission and fees, by matching remittance or settlement lines to orders.

### Which channels can be reconciled?
Any marketplace or store whose remittance or settlement report can be imported, including Amazon, Temu, Woolworths Everyday Market, BIG W Market, Bunnings Marketplace, Kmart Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, Myer Marketplace, THE ICONIC, Lasoo, Temple & Webster, Baby Bunting and Kogan and Shopify.
