# Branded storefronts, connected to your OMS

URL: https://ecomchannels.com/products/storefronts/
Updated: 2026-10-30

> Your own branded online store on the eComChannels storefront engine: SEO and AI-search friendly, with products, prices and stock from the OMS.

The eComChannels storefront engine powers branded web stores. Each store has its own theme and content, while products, prices and stock come directly from the order management system.

## Features

### Search and AI friendly
Server-rendered pages, clean URLs, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, schema.org structured data and llms.txt for AI assistants.

### Fed by the OMS
Products, prices and stock are pushed from the OMS, and web orders flow into the same order queue as your marketplaces.

### Your brand, your theme
Each store has its own Liquid theme and content.

### Checkout and payments
Cart and checkout with online payment providers built in.
